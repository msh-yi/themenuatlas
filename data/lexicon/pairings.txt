# generic: too vague to say anything ("fish", "herbs"). Left out of pairings entirely, but still
#   listed as ingredients and on menus.
# staple: everywhere, so their pairings are obvious (bread + butter, pasta + parmesan). Pairings
#   with them are hidden unless the viewer turns on "Include staples".
# same: two names for one thing on the plate (trout and its roe, fig and fig leaf). Never paired.
#   One pair per line. A parent and its kind (mushroom > porcini in ingredients.txt) are never
#   paired either, without being listed here.
