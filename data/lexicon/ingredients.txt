# Ingredient lexicon for the rule-based extractor (src/menudb/extract/auto.py).
#
#   canonical [> parent] = lang: term, term | lang: term
#
# The canonical name (lowercase, singular, English) is always an English term too.
# `## category` lines set the category for the entries below them. Languages are
# ISO 639-1. Terms are matched case- and accent-insensitively; English, French,
# Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish/Swedish/Norwegian and German plurals
# ending in s/es/x/n/en/e/er are tried automatically, other plurals are listed.
# A longer term wins over a shorter one ("black truffle" over "truffle").
